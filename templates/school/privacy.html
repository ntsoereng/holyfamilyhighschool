1. Responsibility and applicable law

{{ school.name }} is responsible for personal information collected through this website. Contact the school office using our contact page{% if school.email %} or email {{ school.email }}{% endif %} about this policy or a privacy request.

Our handling of personal information is guided by Lesotho’s Data Protection Act, 2011 (Act No. 5 of 2012). The Act addresses lawful processing, purpose limitation, retention, security, access and correction, and transfers outside Lesotho. Relevant provisions include sections 15–26, 29 and 36, and 52. This policy describes this website; separate school records and services may require additional notices.

2. What we collect and why

  • Applications: learner names, date of birth, intake year and entry level, address and district where supplied, current school, boarding choice, guardian name, contact details and consent. We use these to assess and administer admissions, contact the family and maintain an application record. An application reference and internal review notes support follow-up.
  • Enquiries: your name, email, optional phone number, subject, message and consent. We use these to respond, direct your enquiry and record its resolution.
  • Published school content: staff names, roles and approved profiles; student author names, grades, contributions and any approved images. These identify contributors and share school news and learning with the public.
  • Technical and security information: requests may generate server logs containing IP addresses, requested pages, browser details and timestamps. Form protection uses hashed identifiers derived from network addresses; staff sign-in protection records login attempts. These help maintain the service, investigate faults and prevent abuse.

We trim and standardise submitted names and email addresses to improve record accuracy. We use an application identity fingerprint to detect duplicate applications for the same learner and intake. These checks do not decide whether a learner is admitted; school staff review applications.

Required fields are identified on each form. Without them we may be unable to process the application or reply. Please provide only relevant information: do not send passwords, bank details, identity documents, medical records or other sensitive documents through the general enquiry form.

3. Grounds for using information

Our forms ask for agreement to the stated use of the information. Applications require parent or guardian consent. We also process information where necessary for lawful school administration, to meet legal obligations or to protect the website and its users, subject to applicable law. We do not treat browsing the site as permission for unrelated marketing. We do not sell application or enquiry information.

4. Children, photographs and student contributions

A parent or guardian should help a learner complete an application. For student stories, poems and articles, staff publish contributions on students’ behalf; students do not need a website account. Author names and grades appear publicly with the contribution. The school must obtain the appropriate permission before publishing identifiable student work or photographs, including parent or guardian permission where required.

Public pages can be indexed by search engines and copied by others. Please contact the office if a name, image or contribution should be corrected or removed. We can review content on this website, but cannot guarantee removal of copies held independently by other services.

5. Who can access information

Application and enquiry records are available to authorised school staff for their duties. Hosting, maintenance and communications providers may process information where needed to operate the service. Information may also be disclosed when lawfully required or necessary to protect legal rights or safety. Public contributions and profiles are accessible to anyone visiting the website; private form submissions are not published.

External providers may process information outside Lesotho. Any school-arranged overseas transfer must have an appropriate lawful basis and safeguards under section 52 of the Act. Contact us for information about the providers used for your record; we do not imply that all website data is hosted in Lesotho.

6. Retention and security

Applications, correspondence and related notes are retained for admissions and enquiry administration and any applicable recordkeeping, dispute or legal requirements. Accepted applicants’ information may become part of the school’s learner records. Technical records and backups have separate operational lifecycles. We review retention according to purpose and applicable requirements rather than promise one deletion period for every record. Contact the office to ask about a particular record.

The website uses access controls, form validation, submission limits and sign-in protection; production settings require HTTPS and secure authentication cookies. No internet service can guarantee complete security. Report suspected exposure promptly through the office. We will assess incidents and make notifications required by applicable law. Deleting an active record may not immediately remove protected backup copies.

8. Google Maps, Facebook and other links

If a Google Maps embed is enabled on the contact page, loading that map connects your browser to Google. Google may receive your IP address and browser information and use cookies or similar technologies under its own Privacy Policy. You can contact the office for directions instead.

The Facebook navigation item is a normal link, not an embedded social feed or tracking pixel. Following it takes you to Facebook, where Meta’s Privacy Policy applies. Other linked websites also have their own policies. Do not post application details or sensitive learner information in public social media comments.

9. Access, correction and other requests

You may ask to access personal information held about you, correct inaccurate information, or request deletion or object to processing where the law permits. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it for future processing; this does not undo prior lawful processing. Some records may still need to be retained for lawful purposes.

Send your request through the school office, describing the record concerned and, for applications, your reference if available. We may request proportionate identity or guardian-authority checks before disclosing or changing a learner’s information. We will explain any lawful restriction on your request.

The Act provides for complaints to the Data Protection Commission. Its operational arrangements should be verified before lodging a complaint; contact the relevant Lesotho government authority for the current route. You may seek independent advice about available legal remedies.
