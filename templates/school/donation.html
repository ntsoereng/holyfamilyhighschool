Support our school

{{ school.name }} welcomes support from individuals, families, alumni, businesses and friends of the school. Whether you would like to make a financial contribution or offer useful goods and services, please speak to the school office so we can arrange a donation that meets current needs.

{% include 'components/icon.html' with name='shield' %}

Monetary donations

Financial contributions can help the school respond to its priorities. Contact the office to discuss where your support could be most useful, including any particular purpose you have in mind.

The school will confirm the appropriate payment arrangements directly with you. This website does not collect donation payments.

{% include 'components/icon.html' with name='book' %}

In-kind donations

You may wish to offer books, learning materials, equipment, supplies or professional services. Let us know what you would like to contribute, its quantity and condition, and when it would be available.

Please check with the office before purchasing, delivering or sending goods so we can confirm suitability, storage and delivery arrangements.

How to donate

  1. Call or email the school office and explain what you would like to give.
  2. Discuss current needs and agree on the purpose, payment method or delivery details.
  3. Arrange your contribution directly with the school and ask the office about acknowledgement of receipt.
